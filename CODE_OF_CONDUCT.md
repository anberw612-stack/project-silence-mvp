# Code of Conduct

Fortress is a privacy-first research prototype for sensitive health conversations. Contributors must help keep the project careful, humane, and safe for people who may be discussing vulnerable situations.

## Expected Behavior

- Use respectful, patient, and constructive language.
- Treat privacy, consent, and medical safety concerns as first-class engineering feedback.
- Use synthetic examples only in issues, pull requests, tests, screenshots, and documentation.
- Assume private health details can be identifying even when names are removed.
- Make room for contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Posting real private health, patient, clinician, workplace, family, or location-specific narratives.
- Sharing API keys, database URLs, Streamlit secrets, tokens, logs, or screenshots that expose secrets.
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized remarks, or personal attacks.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose medical history, identity, workplace, or personal circumstances.
- Presenting Fortress as a diagnostic, treatment, emergency, or clinician-replacement system.

## Privacy and Medical-Safety Escalation

If an issue, pull request, or discussion contains private health information or credentials, stop public discussion and ask maintainers to remove or redact the content. Do not quote the sensitive material in follow-up comments.

If a contribution changes medical-safety behavior, decoy generation, retrieval, reranking, consent, deletion, or logging, reviewers should ask for a privacy and safety rationale before merging.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete content that violates this code. They may also close issues, block users, or reject contributions that create privacy, safety, or conduct risks.

For now, report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub account contact channels. Use synthetic summaries whenever possible.
